REMEMRO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

REMEMRO ("the app") is a personal scrapbook for special dates, gift ideas and

memories. It is designed to keep your information on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not use

analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Everything you add — events, people's names, notes, gift ideas, budgets,

photos, captions, stickers and settings — is stored only in the app's private

storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

On-device features that never leave your phone:

  • Photo insights label photos you add (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple

Vision on iOS) to suggest stickers and captions.

  • Quote & suggestion model learns from the suggestions you pick.
  • Smart reminder time learns when you usually open the app.

Permissions

  • Photos / Camera — only to add the pictures you choose.
  • Notifications — only to deliver the reminders you set.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We receive no payment

details. Your purchase status is stored on your device and can be restored

through the store.

Optional online ideas

If a future version enables online "Little Inspiration", only the minimal text

needed for a suggestion (for example a first name, interests and budget) would

be sent, never photos. This feature is off in the current version.

Children

REMEMRO is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
